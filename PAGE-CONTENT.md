## Page: Shipping & Delivery

## SHIPPING SMALL PACKAGE

- Orders ship on the next business day after the payment has cleared.
- Orders placed on weekends or holidays ship the next business day.
- We ship via USPS, UPS, or FedEx, and reserve the right to select or change carriers as needed.
- Shipping rates are based on the destination ZIP code and apply to the lower 48 states only.
- Expedited shipping may be available at an additional cost. Buyers must contact us before purchasing to confirm availability. All expedited shipping fees are non-refundable.
- We do not control international shipping costs, taxes, or customs fees.
- Local pickup is available for all items. Buyers must contact us in advance to ensure the order is prepared.

## FREIGHT SHIPPING

- Large items (including but not limited to engines, transmissions, body panels, axles, exhausts, interiors, cradles, etc.) ship via freight.
- Freight orders ship within 1 business days after cleared payment and receipt of required delivery information.
- Freight transit times may take up to 7 business days.
- Locations without a dock or forklift require a liftgate service, which must be disclosed at time of purchase.
- Residential freight delivery must be quoted prior to purchase and typically costs significantly more than commercial delivery.
- Buyers have the option to pick freight from a local freight terminal. If you opt for this option we will provide you with the location of a terminal closest to you. Buyer must show up with proper equipment and identification to pick up the shipment.
- A valid phone number and business name are required with all freight shipments.
- If you do not meet the commercial delivery criteria, you must contact us to obtain an accurate shipping quote.
- In the event that the buyer does not respond to freight carrier attempts to call and schedule, or misses his delivery appointment buyer will be responsible for redelivery fee which range between $100-$300 depending on the size and weight of the package.
- All freight shipments must be unpackaged and thoroughly inspected before signing for delivery.
- If an item arrives damaged, you must refuse delivery for us to be able to refund you. If you accept and sign for delivery your order will no longer be eligible for a refund.

## ENGINE WARRANTY TERMS

- Engines must be professionally installed by a properly licensed or certified automotive repair facility. Proof of professional installation may be required for warranty consideration.
- Components attached to the engine are provided for convenience and are not guaranteed or covered unless specifically identified as covered on the invoice. These may include, but are not limited to, intake and exhaust manifolds, turbochargers/superchargers, harmonic balancers, fuel injectors, fuel system components, flywheels/flexplates, brackets, water pumps, alternators, starters, A/C compressors, wiring harnesses, sensors, switches, thermostats, and emissions equipment.
- Prior to installation, the installer must replace or service all applicable maintenance items, including fluids, filters, gaskets, seals, spark plugs, and timing components when required by the manufacturer's recommended installation procedures.
- 417 Motoring LLC ships engines and other applicable components with fluids drained. The purchaser and installer are responsible for filling the engine and all related systems with the correct type and quantity of oil, coolant, and other required fluids before operation.
- The cooling system must be inspected, properly filled, and confirmed to be functioning correctly before the engine is operated.
- Overheating, oil starvation, improper lubrication, contamination, improper tuning, incorrect installation, or continued operation after a warning condition may void warranty coverage.
- Disassembly, modification, or unauthorized removal of cylinder heads, internal engine components, or the engine block without prior authorization from 417 Motoring LLC will void the warranty.

## TRANSMISSION WARRANTY TERMS

- All applicable seals, gaskets, filters, and fluids should be replaced or serviced before installation.
- Transmissions are shipped with fluids drained. The purchaser and installer are responsible for filling the transmission with the manufacturer's specified type and quantity of fluid before operation.
- Transmission coolers, lines, and related fluid systems must be properly flushed or replaced as necessary to prevent contamination from the previous transmission.
- Required programming, adaptation, relearning, coding, or calibration procedures must be completed according to the vehicle manufacturer's specifications.
- Damage to gears, housings, cases, shafts, or other components resulting from improper installation, insufficient lubrication, abuse, misuse, incorrect programming, or vehicle related problems is not covered.
- Transmission control modules (TCMs), sensors, switches, wiring, and other electronic components included with a transmission are considered ancillary components and are not covered unless specifically stated otherwise.
- Opening, disassembling, modifying, or tampering with a transmission without prior authorization from 417 Motoring LLC will void warranty coverage.
- Fluid leaks, seals, gaskets, and labor expenses are not covered under the standard parts warranty.

## AXLES, DIFFERENTIALS, CARRIERS & TRANSFER CASES

- The purchaser and installer are responsible for inspecting and replacing seals, gaskets, and other serviceable components as necessary before installation.
- Seals, brake components, sensors, switches, actuators, and shift motors are not covered unless specifically stated otherwise.
- Components will be shipped with fluids drained. The purchaser and installer are responsible for adding the manufacturer's required lubricant or fluid in the correct quantity before operation.
- Damage caused by insufficient or incorrect fluid, improper installation, abuse, misuse, incorrect gear ratios, or incompatible drivetrain components is excluded from warranty coverage.

## SHEET METAL & BODY PARTS

- Used body and sheet metal components are sold in the condition represented in the listing and accompanying photographs.
- Unless specifically stated otherwise, cosmetic imperfections consistent with used automotive components—including scratches, chips, dents, surface corrosion, paint wear, fading, and other normal signs of use are not considered warrantable defects.
- Damage or deterioration resulting from accidents, improper installation, modification, abuse, fire, hail, wind, flooding, environmental exposure, or other external causes is not covered.

## AIRBAGS & SUPPLEMENTAL RESTRAINT SYSTEM (SRS) COMPONENTS

- Recycled airbags and other SRS components sold by 417 Motoring LLC are sold AS IS. No standard parts warranty is provided for these components.
- Airbags and SRS components must be inspected and installed by a properly qualified professional in accordance with the vehicle manufacturer's procedures and all applicable laws and regulations.

- The purchaser assumes responsibility for ensuring that any recycled SRS component is appropriate for the intended vehicle and is professionally installed, programmed, and tested as required.
